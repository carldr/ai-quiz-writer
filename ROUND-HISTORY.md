# Round history

One row per past round, newest quiz first. Full questions are in OLD-QUIZZES.md and quizzes/*/quiz.md. A new quiz's rounds go in at the top when it is rendered.

Format is how teams answer, named as in the `Format:` line of `quiz.md`: `picture`, `multiple-choice`, `true-false` or `open`. Two past formats have no `quiz.md` equivalent: `audio`, for played clips, and `map`, for marking places on a map. A round's gimmick, such as anagrams or "X or Y?", goes under Theme / gimmick.
