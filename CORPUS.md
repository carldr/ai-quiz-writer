# Corpus

Rounds and questions good enough to use, each with the notes that say why. Two
sources feed it: `/vet-suggestions` adds the suggestions that pass vetting, and
`/new-quiz` adds rounds drafted for a quiz and passed over for another round.
Take themes and questions from here when proposing rounds, rather than starting
from nothing.

A round rejected for a fault does not belong here. If it was dropped because its
questions gave away their answers, its images could not be sourced, or its facts
would not verify, it is gone — do not record it.

**Everything below was checked when it was added, and not since.** Answers were
verified then, sometimes against a single source, and none has been deduped
against quizzes written since. Re-verify every answer and re-run the dedupe
before using anything here.

Delete an entry once it has been used in a quiz.

## Open and themed rounds

### Around the World

Drafted 2026-08-31 for the 2026-09-01 quiz, which took Food and Drink instead.
Geography. Expected 5.55 out of 10.

1. Which African country is completely surrounded by a single other country? — **Lesotho (enclaved by South Africa; the only other enclaved states are San Marino and Vatican City)**
2. Every national flag in the world is a rectangle or a square, except one. Which country's flag is not? — **Nepal (a concave pentagon of two stacked pennants)**
3. The Danube flows through four national capital cities. Name two of them. — **Any two of Vienna, Bratislava, Budapest and Belgrade**
4. Which is the only country crossed by both the Equator and the Tropic of Capricorn? — **Brazil**
5. What is the largest lake in the world by surface area? — **The Caspian Sea (about 371,000 square kilometres)**
6. Which country has the world's largest population? — **India (passed China on 1 July 2023, on UN figures)**
7. Which island is divided between three sovereign countries? — **Borneo (Indonesia, Malaysia and Brunei)**
8. The only break in the road link running from Alaska to southern Argentina is a roadless stretch of rainforest and swamp about 100 km across. Which two countries does it lie between? — **Panama and Colombia (the Darién Gap; both countries needed for the point)**
9. Which country's territory spans five geographical time zones, but sets every clock in it to the same time? — **China (the whole country runs on UTC+8)**
10. Which country shares a land border with both Norway and North Korea? — **Russia (196 km with Norway, 17 km with North Korea)**

Before reuse: question 6 turns on a population ranking that changes, so re-check
it. Question 7 avoids the word "only" deliberately — Wikipedia does not assert
that Borneo is the only three-country island, and a team may offer Cyprus, which
does not qualify.

### Science and Nature

Drafted 2026-08-31 for the 2026-09-01 quiz, which took Food and Drink instead.
Expected 5.3 out of 10. Disciplines: chemistry 2, physics 2, human body 2, space
1, animals 1, plants 1, weather 1.

1. Two letters of the alphabet do not appear in the name of any chemical element. Name both. — **J and Q**
2. Stainless steel is iron alloyed with at least 10.5% of which other metal, the one that gives it its resistance to rust? — **Chromium**
3. Absolute zero, the lowest temperature possible, is zero on the kelvin scale. What is it in degrees Celsius? — **−273.15 °C (accept −273)**
4. What name is given to the change in pitch you hear as an ambulance siren passes you? — **The Doppler effect**
5. Which planet takes 243 Earth days to turn once on its axis, longer than the 225 days it takes to go round the Sun? — **Venus**
6. The smallest bone in the human body sits in the middle ear. What is it called? Either the Latin or the English name will do. — **The stapes, or stirrup**
7. Which organ of the human body produces insulin? — **The pancreas**
8. Which is the largest living species of lizard? — **The Komodo dragon**
9. Potatoes, tomatoes, aubergines and chilli peppers all belong to which plant family, which also includes tobacco? — **The nightshades (Solanaceae)**
10. The UK's highest ever recorded temperature was set at Coningsby in Lincolnshire on 19 July 2022. To the nearest degree Celsius, what was it? — **40 °C (40.3 °C exactly)**

Before reuse: fix the marking on question 10. The draft accepted "40 or 41",
which contradicts the question — 40.3 to the nearest degree is 40. Question 10
also needs re-checking against later heat records. Replace question 7 as well:
the 2026-09-01 quiz asks which organ makes insulin in its general knowledge
round, so that fact is spent.
