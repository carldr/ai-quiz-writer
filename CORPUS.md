### J. M. Barrie and Quality Street

Quality Street chocolates, launched by Mackintosh's in 1936, are named after J. M. Barrie's 1901 play *Quality Street*. — **J. M. Barrie**

- Asked as: "Quality Street chocolates were named after a 1901 play by which author, better known for Peter Pan?" About 60%.
- Harder: "What links Peter Pan and Quality Street?" About 35%.
- Accuracy: Barrie wrote the play, not the chocolates; the soldier and lady on the tin are the play's Captain Valentine Brown and Phoebe Throssel.
- Marking: for the "what links" form, the answer sheet spells out the play.
- Why: a household tin with a literary origin few know.
- Vetted 2026-09-23.

### Most streamed song by a Welsh artist

Total Eclipse of the Heart by Bonnie Tyler passed a billion Spotify streams, the most for any Welsh artist (BBC, 26 January 2026). — **Total Eclipse of the Heart (Bonnie Tyler)**

- Asked as: "Which song is the most streamed on Spotify by a Welsh artist, passing a billion plays?" About 40%.
- Easier: ask for the singer as multiple choice against Tom Jones, Duffy and Marina. About 55%.
- Accuracy: say "by a Welsh artist", not "Welsh song", which reads as Welsh-language. Marina's Primadonna was next, at about 573m.
- Faults: depends on a Spotify ranking; the song was written by the American Jim Steinman.
- Why: teams can reason through the Welsh singers they know.
- Vetted 2026-09-23.

### As You Like It and Soulton Hall

Soulton Hall, near Wem, claims its builder Sir Rowland Hill inspired "Old Sir Rowland" in As You Like It, and Soulton Wood the Forest of Arden. — **As You Like It**

- Asked as: "Soulton Hall, near Wem, claims its builder Sir Rowland Hill inspired 'Old Sir Rowland' in which Shakespeare comedy, set in the Forest of Arden?" About 55%.
- Easier: multiple choice against Twelfth Night and Much Ado About Nothing. About 80%.
- Harder: drop the Forest of Arden.
- Accuracy: word it as the hall's claim; it is not scholarly consensus, and no source says the play was written in Wem.
- Why: a local link most locals won't know.
- Vetted 2026-09-23.

### Gala pie

A gala pie is a long, loaf-shaped pork pie with hard-boiled egg running through its centre. — **Hard-boiled egg**

- Asked as: "A gala pie is a long pork pie with what running through its centre?" About 65%.
- Easier: "What is a gala pie?" as multiple choice against a steak and ale pie and a fish pie. About 90%.
- Marking: accept "egg" alone.
- Why: a British buffet staple most teams have eaten.
- Vetted 2026-09-23.

### SPAM

The name SPAM is usually said to be short for "spiced ham"; Hormel has never confirmed a meaning. — **Spiced ham**

- Asked as: "The name SPAM is usually said to be a shortening of which two words?" About 50%.
- Accuracy: keep "usually said to be"; "what does SPAM stand for?" has no answer you can mark.
- Why: "Specially Processed American Meat" is the wrong reflex answer.
- Vetted 2026-09-23.

### Montenegro and Kenya

Montenegro means "black mountain", after Mount Lovćen; Kenya is named after Mount Kenya, Africa's second-highest mountain. — **Montenegro**, **Kenya**

- Asked as: "Which European country's name means 'black mountain'?" About 70%.
- Also: "Which African country takes its name from the continent's second-highest mountain?" About 75%.
- Accuracy: never "the only country named after a mountain"; both qualify.
- Why: teams can work Montenegro out from "monte" and "negro".
- Vetted 2026-09-23.

### The Wizard of Oz

The Wizard of Oz (1939) opens in sepia Kansas, switches to Technicolor in Oz, and goes back to sepia for the ending. — **The Wizard of Oz**

- Asked as: "Which 1939 film switches from sepia to Technicolor partway through?" About 50%.
- Easier: name Kansas, or Dorothy opening the door.
- Harder: "What does The Wizard of Oz end in, sepia or colour?" Sepia; a near-trick, since the reflex answer is colour.
- Accuracy: say sepia, not black and white; it does not end in colour.
- Why: the most famous colour switch in film.
- Vetted 2026-09-23.

### Stopping distance at 70 mph

The Highway Code's typical overall stopping distance at 70 mph is 96 metres: 21 m thinking plus 75 m braking. — **96 metres**

- Asked as: "According to the Highway Code, what is the typical overall stopping distance for a car at 70 mph? a) 36 metres b) 96 metres c) 1 kilometre" About 75%.
- Harder: open, or with closer options.
- Accuracy: keep "typical"; the figures are for a dry road.
- Why: most teams include a driver, and the silly option keeps it accessible.
- Vetted 2026-09-23.

### The Baader-Meinhof phenomenon

The effect of noticing something everywhere just after learning about it is the Baader-Meinhof phenomenon, also called the frequency illusion. — **The Baader-Meinhof phenomenon (or frequency illusion)**

- Asked as: "What name is given to the effect where, having just learned about something new, you suddenly seem to notice it everywhere?" About 40%.
- Easier: multiple choice against the Mandela effect and the Dunning-Kruger effect. About 85%.
- Marking: accept both names; "confirmation bias", "selective attention" and "recency illusion" are wrong.
- Why: everyone has experienced it; few know its name.
- Vetted 2026-09-23.

### Largest landlocked country

Kazakhstan, at 2,724,900 km², is the largest landlocked country in the world. — **Kazakhstan**

- Asked as: "What is the largest landlocked country in the world?" About 60%.
- Easier: "Which Central Asian country…" About 90%.
- Accuracy: its Caspian coast doesn't count; the Caspian is itself landlocked.
- Why: Mongolia is a plausible wrong answer.
- Vetted 2026-09-23.

### Territory on all seven continents

Counting overseas territories and Antarctic claims, the UK has territory on all seven continents; France has six, and none in Asia. — **The United Kingdom**

- Asked as: "Counting its overseas territories and Antarctic claims, which country has territory on all seven continents?" About 50%, a mild trick, since many teams reach for France.
- Accuracy: keep the qualifying clause. Asia is Akrotiri and Dhekelia on Cyprus, Africa is St Helena, Oceania is Pitcairn.
- Faults: the Antarctic claim is suspended under the Antarctic Treaty, and the Asian box depends on counting Cyprus as Asia. The Chagos handover, on hold in September 2026, would not change the answer.
- Why: the answer surprises people.
- Vetted 2026-09-23.

### The angels' share

The spirit lost to evaporation from the cask as whisky matures is the angels' share. — **The angels' share**

- Vetted 2026-09-23.
