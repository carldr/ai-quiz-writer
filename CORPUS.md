questions from here when proposing rounds, rather than starting from nothing.

verified then, sometimes against a single source, and none has been deduped
against quizzes written since. Re-verify every answer and re-run the dedupe
before using anything here.

## Questions

### J. M. Barrie and Quality Street

1. Quality Street chocolates were named after a 1901 play by which author, better known for Peter Pan? — **J. M. Barrie**

Vetted from SUGGESTIONS.md on 2026-09-23. Chance: about 60%; about 35% as a bare "What links Peter Pan and Quality Street?". A household tin with a literary origin few know; the soldier and lady on the tin are the play's Captain Valentine Brown and Phoebe Throssel. Faults: the note said Barrie wrote both; he wrote the play, and Mackintosh's named the chocolates after it in 1936. As a bare "what links", the answer sheet must spell out the play.

### Most streamed song by a Welsh artist

1. Which song is the most streamed on Spotify by a Welsh artist, passing a billion plays? — **Total Eclipse of the Heart (Bonnie Tyler)**

Vetted from SUGGESTIONS.md on 2026-09-23. Chance: about 40%; about 55% as multiple choice on the artist, against Tom Jones, Duffy and Marina. Teams can reason through the Welsh singers they know. Source: BBC, 26 January 2026. Faults: say "by a Welsh artist", not "Welsh song", which reads as Welsh-language. It depends on a Spotify ranking; Marina's Primadonna was next, at about 573m. The song was written by the American Jim Steinman.

### As You Like It and Soulton Hall

1. Soulton Hall, near Wem, claims its builder Sir Rowland Hill inspired "Old Sir Rowland" in which Shakespeare comedy, set in the Forest of Arden? — **As You Like It**

Vetted from SUGGESTIONS.md on 2026-09-23. Chance: about 55%; about 80% as multiple choice against Twelfth Night and Much Ado About Nothing. A local link most locals won't know, and the Arden clue makes it reachable. Faults: the note said the play was written in Wem, which no source supports. The link is the hall's own claim, not scholarly consensus, so the question must be worded as a claim.

### Gala pie

1. A gala pie is a long pork pie with what running through its centre? — **Hard-boiled egg**

Vetted from SUGGESTIONS.md on 2026-09-23. Chance: about 65%; about 90% as multiple choice against a steak and ale pie and a fish pie. A British buffet staple most teams have eaten. Faults: the note's "What is gala pie?" has a descriptive answer that is hard to mark, so the question asks for the egg. Accept "egg" alone.

### SPAM

1. The name SPAM is usually said to be a shortening of which two words? — **Spiced ham**

Vetted from SUGGESTIONS.md on 2026-09-23. Chance: about 50%. Most people have heard a backronym such as "Specially Processed American Meat", which is the wrong reflex. Faults: the note asked what SPAM stands for, which has no markable answer; Hormel says the meaning is known only to a few former executives. "Usually said to be" covers that.

### Montenegro

1. Which European country's name means "black mountain"? — **Montenegro**

Vetted from SUGGESTIONS.md on 2026-09-23. Chance: about 70%. Teams can work it out from "monte" and "negro". Faults: the note asked for the only country named after a mountain, which is false: Kenya is named after Mount Kenya. That gives a second question: "Which African country takes its name from the continent's second-highest mountain?" (Kenya, about 75%).

### Paul McCartney's instrument

1. What was Paul McCartney's main instrument in The Beatles? — **Bass guitar**

Vetted from SUGGESTIONS.md on 2026-09-23. Chance: 90–95%. An easy question for the easy end of a round. Faults: he also played piano, lead guitar and drums on some tracks, so "main" must stay in the wording.

### The Men in Black Neuralyzer

1. In the Men in Black films, what is the name of the flashing device agents use to wipe people's memories? — **The Neuralyzer**

Vetted from SUGGESTIONS.md on 2026-09-23. Chance: about 50%. Most people remember the prop; fewer know its name. Faults: spellings vary ("Neuralizer"), so mark on sound.

### The Wizard of Oz

1. Which 1939 film opens in sepia-tinted Kansas and switches to Technicolor when its heroine opens a door? — **The Wizard of Oz**

Vetted from SUGGESTIONS.md on 2026-09-23. Chance: about 95%. The most famous colour switch in film. Faults: the note said it starts in black and white and ends in colour; the Kansas scenes are sepia, and the film ends in sepia, back in Kansas. A harder version is a near-trick question: "The Wizard of Oz begins in sepia and turns to colour in Oz. What does it end in?" (sepia, about 40%; the reflex is colour).

### Stopping distance at 70 mph

1. According to the Highway Code, what is the typical overall stopping distance for a car at 70 mph? a) 36 metres b) 96 metres c) 1 kilometre — **b) 96 metres**

Vetted from SUGGESTIONS.md on 2026-09-23. Chance: about 75%. Most teams include a driver, and the silly 1 km option keeps it accessible. 96 m is 21 m thinking plus 75 m braking. Faults: these are typical dry-road figures; keep "typical" in the wording.

### The Baader-Meinhof phenomenon

1. What name is given to the effect where, having just learned about something new, you suddenly seem to notice it everywhere? — **The Baader-Meinhof phenomenon (or frequency illusion)**

Vetted from SUGGESTIONS.md on 2026-09-23. Chance: about 40%; about 85% as multiple choice against the Mandela effect and the Dunning-Kruger effect. Everyone has experienced it; few know its name. Faults: accept both names; do not accept "confirmation bias", "selective attention" or "recency illusion".

### Largest landlocked country

1. What is the largest landlocked country in the world? — **Kazakhstan**

Vetted from SUGGESTIONS.md on 2026-09-23. Chance: about 60%. Most teams know Kazakhstan but not that it has no sea coast, and Mongolia is a plausible wrong answer. Faults: the note's "Central Asian" hint gives it away (about 90%), so it is dropped. A team may raise the Caspian coast; the Caspian is itself landlocked.

### Territory on all seven continents

1. Counting its overseas territories and Antarctic claims, which country has territory on all seven continents? — **The United Kingdom**

Vetted from SUGGESTIONS.md on 2026-09-23. Chance: about 50%. Many teams will reach for France, which has six and none in Asia, so it works as a mild trick question. Asia is Akrotiri and Dhekelia on Cyprus, Africa is St Helena, Oceania is Pitcairn. Faults: the Antarctic claim is suspended under the Antarctic Treaty, and the Asian box depends on counting Cyprus as Asia. The Chagos handover, on hold as of September 2026, would not change the answer.

### The angels' share

1. In whisky making, what name is given to the spirit lost to evaporation from the cask as it matures? — **The angels' share**

Vetted from SUGGESTIONS.md on 2026-09-23. Chance: about 80%. A well-known phrase many teams know or can work out. Faults: "the devil's cut" is the spirit soaked into the wood, a different thing.
